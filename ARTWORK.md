are covered here.

**What counts as the pictures and the music:**

- everything in `themes/`: the scene generators, their data and their helpers;
- everything in `cards/` and `assets/`;
- every `scene-*.js` and `photos.ts` file in `plugins/*/hooks/`, and every file in `plugins/*/music/`.

Anything not plainly code that is in doubt is covered here too.

## Reaching

**Artwork by @samdape, used with permission. All rights stay with the artist.** The hands are
@samdape's dot rendering of the hands in Michelangelo's *Creation of Adam*. The fresco itself is
public domain; this permission is about @samdape's rendering.
For any use beyond the above, ask @samdape.

On windows wider than a laptop the forearms carry on past the artwork's left and right edges. That
part is **not** @samdape's: it is our extension, traced from the public-domain fresco in the same
dots (`themes/reaching/fresco-arms.json`, made by `themes/reaching/trace-arms.py`).
